REVENUE MONSTER MANUAL ADJUSMENT

Author: Zul-IT • Last Updated: 2026-08-06

Blank Store ID / Store Name

If you find a record with blank Store ID or Store Name, you can find the reason in Payouts menu.

Payment
└ Payouts

Choose specific date for MANUAL ADJUSTMENT. for this case, you can choose 22th July 2026

Left click on the row to find details.

You will see 2 type of manual adjustment Debit Note and Credit Note

For Debit Note, left click on Download to see details and copy Trx ID: 17835805031463

Paste copied Trx ID on:

Payment
└ Transactions

And you will find the Trx ID 17835805031463 was happening at outlet SD
